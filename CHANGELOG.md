## 5.1 — Liberty Isle

Liberty Isle is one mountain island beside Turtle Cove: a sand beach, a grass lowland, and a faceted rock peak, with one path network that follows the ground. The History Time-Tunnel is a stone arch cut into the mountainside, with a dark room, two lanterns, and a sign. The dock map is a low table with a contiguous United States silhouette, five colored regions, and Alaska and Hawaii insets. The Statue of Liberty, Gateway Arch, Golden Gate Bridge, Liberty Bell, Mount Rushmore, and the Alamo are built so each silhouette reads at a glance. A Stars and Stripes hangs from the top of the pole beside the dock. Turtle Cove is restored. Five stations: Map Puzzle Dock, History Time-Tunnel, Road Trip Rally, Landmark Builder, and Fifty-State Quiz Show.
