## Mount Rushmore photograph

The carved face on Liberty Isle uses a photograph of Mount Rushmore National Memorial.

- Source: National Park Service Image Gallery, via Wikimedia Commons, [File:Mount Rushmore.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Rushmore.jpg)
- Description: the four presidential heads, left to right George Washington, Thomas Jefferson, Theodore Roosevelt, and Abraham Lincoln
- License: public domain, PD-USGov. It is a work of the United States National Park Service, prepared by a federal employee as part of official duties (17 U.S.C. § 105). Wikimedia Commons also marks the file as free of known copyright restrictions.

The game crops that photograph to the four faces, uses it as the cliff color, and turns its brightness into a height field and a normal map so the relief is lit by the scene.
