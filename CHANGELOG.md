# Changelog

## 5.1 — Liberty Isle

Liberty Isle now occupies the old Turtle Cove landmass (the largest outer island that is not the lair), with a mountain, a cave tunnel, and a walkable shore. The small extra island beside the lighthouse is gone. Five stations: Map Puzzle Dock, History Time-Tunnel, Road Trip Rally, Landmark Builder, and Fifty-State Quiz Show. History questions, era rooms, landmark stories, and state facts come from `content/liberty-isle/history-pack.json`. The only event before 1500 is Columbus's 1492 voyage. Coins, stars, fuel prices, and older save fields are kept. Progress is stored on `state.v51`. The dock map is a United States silhouette with the five regions inside it. The Statue of Liberty, the Gateway Arch, and the Golden Gate Bridge are built so their silhouettes read at a glance. A Stars and Stripes flies at the Liberty dock. The compass names the nearest island the captain has not visited yet. State capitals and land borders were checked against the standard lists.
