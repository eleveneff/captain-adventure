A new boat-reachable island, Liberty Isle, with five stations: Map Puzzle Dock, History Time-Tunnel, Road Trip Rally, Landmark Builder, and Fifty-State Quiz Show. Coins, stars, fuel prices, and older save fields are kept. Progress is stored on `state.v51`.
